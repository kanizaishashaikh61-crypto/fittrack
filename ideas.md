# FitTrack design brief

## Design movement
Editorial fitness dashboard meets athletic control room: a calm slate canvas with bright data accents and tactile cards.

## Core principles
- Make today's next action obvious: log, hydrate, or adjust a goal.
- Use color as meaning: lime for goal progress, cyan for hydration, coral for nutrition.
- Keep high-density information scannable with generous spacing and strong hierarchy.
- Every mutation gives immediate visual feedback and remains recoverable through edit/delete.

## Color philosophy
Base: #0F172A slate with layered #111C31 and #17233A surfaces. Lime #84CC16 signals active goals and success. Cyan #06B6D4 owns hydration. Coral #FB7185 and orange #F97316 distinguish macro and meal information. Text uses warm white and cool muted slate.

## Layout paradigm
Desktop two-column dashboard: a wide main activity stream and a compact right rail for hydration/workouts. On mobile, collapse to one column with a sticky compact header and full-width cards.

## Signature elements
A donut-style calorie gauge, oversized metric typography, thin progress bars, pill tags, and a “Today’s rhythm” micro timeline.

## Interaction philosophy
Optimistic, low-friction controls. Quick-add buttons are one tap. Forms default to the most common meal and activity choices. Modals are keyboard-friendly and dismissible.

## Animation
Use short ease-out transitions for cards, gauge progress, progress bars, hover lift, and toast feedback. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

## Typography system
Use system UI typography with a display feel from bold tracking-tight headings, medium-weight labels, and tabular-nums for metrics.

## Brand essence
FitTrack is a daily coach that turns food, water, and movement into a clear next step.

## Brand voice
Encouraging, precise, never preachy. Short labels, concrete numbers, and small nudges.

## Wordmark/logo
A compact outlined pulse-ring with a rising lime checkmark, paired with the FitTrack wordmark.

## Signature brand color
Lime green #84CC16.
